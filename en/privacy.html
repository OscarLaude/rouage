Privacy policy.

Rouage doesn't collect any personal data. No account, no ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your subscriptions stay on your iPhone.

Data collected
None

Data stored on your iPhone

The subscriptions you enter (names, amounts, dates, notes, icons and chosen photos) are saved only in the app, on your device. The developer never has access to them. They may be included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) depending on your iOS settings; those backups are managed by Apple.

Internet connections

The app connects to show service logos, through the provider Logo.dev. It then sends the domain name or the name of the service (for example "netflix.com"). As with any internet request, your IP address is passed on to this provider, which processes it under its privacy policy. No other information, no identifier and no amount is sent. The app may also download exchange rates (see below).

Exchange rates

If a subscription uses a currency other than the display currency, the app downloads the public file of the European Central Bank's reference rates, at most twice a day. The file contains every currency: the ECB doesn't know which ones you use. Only your IP address is passed on, as with any internet request. If you use a single currency, no request is made.

Notifications

Payment reminders are local notifications, scheduled on your iPhone. No server is involved. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Photos

If you choose a photo as an icon, the app only receives the selected image through the iOS picker. It has no access to your photo library. The cropped image stays on your device.

Backup and export

Backups (.json) and exports (.csv) are created only when you ask and saved wherever you choose (Files, iCloud Drive…). The app never sends them anywhere by itself. A backup contains all your subscriptions, icons and photos included: keep it somewhere safe. Restoring only reads the file you select.

Tips

Optional tips are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple. The app neither receives nor keeps any payment information, and a tip unlocks no feature. Supporter badges are computed on your iPhone from your Apple purchase history; nothing is sent.

Retention and deletion

Your data is kept as long as the app is installed. You can delete a subscription, use "Erase all data" in the app's settings, or uninstall the app: everything is then removed from the device.

Your rights

Since the developer processes no personal data, there is none to access, correct or delete. For any question, write to oscarlaudepro@gmail.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or your local authority.

Children

The app collects no data, including from minors.

Changes

If a future feature involves any data collection (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before it ships and your consent will be requested before any collection.
