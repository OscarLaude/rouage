Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. There's no account and no server: the developer has no access to any of your information.

How do I move through my month on the dial?

Press and hold the dial on the Overview screen, then slide your finger around it: each day appears in the center with its payments.

I don't get reminders.

In Rouage, open Settings › Notifications: the "Reminders" switch must be on, and a button tells you if iOS blocks them. Also check that a reminder is set for the subscription (subscription › Edit › Reminder). Reminders arrive at the time chosen in these settings (9 AM by default); "Send a test notification" lets you check.

Are the suggested amounts accurate?

Suggested prices for common services are indicative and may have changed. Check them against your contract: Rouage shows the amounts you enter and never connects to a bank account or provider.

How do I back up or switch iPhones?

Settings › Back up creates a complete file (icons and photos included): save it in Files or iCloud Drive. On the new iPhone, use Settings › Restore a backup, then choose "Add" or "Replace everything".

How do I open my data in a spreadsheet?

Settings › Export for a spreadsheet (CSV).

How do I erase all my data?

In the app: Settings › Erase all data. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.
